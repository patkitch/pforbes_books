Apply to be a Guest Featured Artist

Apply to be considered for one or two gallery walls. The cost is $100 per wall ($200 for two walls), payable when the original artwork is hung. The gallery will review your application and contact you with a decision. Submitting an application does not reserve a wall.

Use the application to provide your contact information, primary medium, requested wall count, preferred months, and either a link to your original artwork or up to five images. Uploaded images are reviewed privately by the gallery.

Show information and gallery terms

  • A Guest Featured Artist displays original artwork on one or two of the front Featured Artist walls for approximately four weeks.
  • Original artwork is installed at 11:00 a.m. on the Monday before the Third Friday opening reception. The display committee will assist with installation.
  • Original artwork must be created by the Guest Featured Artist and ready for display, framed or on gallery-wrapped canvas with hanging hardware in place.
  • Each piece needs an Images Art Gallery title card. Prints of original artwork may be displayed in a bin; prints must be clearly labeled with the artist's name and price. Note cards may also be displayed in the Featured Artist area.
  • The gallery will advertise the opening reception to its email list. The Guest Featured Artist is responsible for promoting the show to their own contacts.
  • The Guest Featured Artist is expected to attend the Third Friday opening reception from 5:00–8:00 p.m. and greet guests and patrons.
  • The Guest Featured Artist provides light refreshments and wine for the reception. The artist may instead pay $75 for Images Art Gallery to provide the food and wine setup.
  • Images Art Gallery handles sales of original artwork, prints, and note cards, collects and remits sales tax, and pays the artist 70% of sold artwork. The gallery retains 30%. The contract states that the same arrangement applies to commissions resulting from the display.
  • The artist is encouraged to replace sold original artwork with another piece.
  • Original artwork is removed at 11:00 a.m. on the Sunday before the following month's Third Friday.

These details summarize the gallery's existing Guest Featured Artist contract; the contract itself is not being changed. The signed contract governs the arrangement.

Important: An application remains pending until the gallery reviews it. A wall is reserved only after approval. Payment remains due when the original artwork is hung under the existing contract.
