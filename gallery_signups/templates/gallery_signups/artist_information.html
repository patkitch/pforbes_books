Apply to be a Guest Featured Artist

Guest artists may request one or two gallery walls. The cost is $100 per wall ($200 for two walls), payable when the artwork is hung. Applying does not reserve a wall. Your request will be reviewed by the gallery; if approved, the selected wall or walls will be reserved and your name will appear on the calendar.

Use the application to provide your contact information, primary medium, requested wall count, preferred months, and either a link to your artwork or up to five images. The gallery will contact you after reviewing your application.

Show information and gallery terms

  • A guest Featured Artist displays work on one or two of the front Featured Artist walls for approximately four weeks.
  • The artist typically installs work on the Monday before the Third Friday opening reception. The display committee will assist with installation.
  • Work must be created by the guest artist and ready for display, framed or on gallery-wrapped canvas with hanging hardware in place.
  • Each work needs an Images Art Gallery title card. Prints of original artwork may be displayed in a bin; prints must be clearly labeled with the artist name and price. Note cards may also be displayed in the Featured Artist area.
  • The gallery will advertise the opening reception to its email list. The guest artist is responsible for promoting the show to their own contacts.
  • The artist is expected to attend the Third Friday opening reception, 5–9 pm, and greet guests and patrons.
  • The guest artist provides light refreshments and wine for the reception. The artist may instead pay $75 for Images Art Gallery to provide the food and wine setup.
  • Images Art Gallery handles sales of artwork, prints, and note cards, collects and remits sales tax, and pays the artist 70% of sold artwork. The gallery retains 30%. The contract states the same arrangement applies to commissions resulting from the display.
  • The artist is encouraged to replace sold artwork with another piece. Work remains on display until the next month's Featured Artist hangs their work.
  • The artist is responsible for removing their work when the show ends, typically after the second Saturday of the following month.

These details summarize the gallery's existing Guest Featured Artist contract; the contract itself is not being changed. The signed contract governs the arrangement.

Important: A request remains pending until the gallery reviews it. A wall is reserved only after approval. Payment remains due at hanging under the existing contract.
